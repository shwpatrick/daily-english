### Article

On May 27, 2025, Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense reported a significant escalation in Chinese military operations near the island. Within a few hours, 61 sorties by aircraft from the People's Liberation Army (PLA) and multiple Chinese naval vessels were detected in the vicinity of Taiwan. This surge in military presence is viewed as a show of force by Beijing amid rising tensions between China and Taiwan. The increased activities underscore heightened geopolitical strains in the Taiwan Strait and raise concerns about regional stability and the potential for broader conflict.

The international community has expressed apprehension over these developments, emphasizing the need for dialogue and peaceful resolution to avoid further escalation. Analysts suggest that such maneuvers could be aimed at testing Taiwan's defenses and gauging international reactions.

### Vocabulary

1. **Sortie** – A deployment or dispatch of troops, aircraft, or ships from a strongpoint.

3. **Geopolitical** – Relating to politics, especially international relations, as influenced by geographical factors.
4. **Strain** – Pressure or tension resulting from demanding circumstances.
5. **Apprehension** – Anxiety or fear that something bad or unpleasant will happen.
